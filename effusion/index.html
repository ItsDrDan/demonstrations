🧪 Try this experiment

1. Choose H₂ for Gas A and O₂ for Gas B. Predict which escapes faster and by what factor.

2. Start the simulation and compare the curves and particle counts.

3. Reset, change the gases, then test helium and xenon. Does changing temperature alter their ratio?

Important: Effusion means particles escape through an opening small relative to their mean free path, into a lower-pressure region. This is an educational, idealized two-dimensional particle model; the graph is stochastic and does not produce laboratory-precision values.
